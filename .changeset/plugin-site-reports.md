Loopress Full can be connected to the Loopress console: paste a site token under Settings, Console, and the site reports its WordPress, PHP and Loopress versions, installed plugins, pending updates and Site Health counts twice a day. Saving the token sends a first report and shows whether it was accepted. Disconnect, a settings reset or uninstalling the plugin deletes the token and stops the reports. Loopress Light never sends anything.
