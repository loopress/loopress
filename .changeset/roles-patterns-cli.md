New `lps role` commands for user roles declared as `roles/<slug>.json` files, and `lps pattern` commands for block patterns written as `patterns/<slug>.html` files (block markup under a `title`/`categories` header, registered as `loopress/<slug>`, Loopress Full only): `push`, `pull`, `list`, `rm`, `diff` and `rollback` for both. `lps push`, `lps pull` and `lps validate` include them, patterns pushed before templates and pages.
