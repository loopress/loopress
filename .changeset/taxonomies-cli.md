New `lps taxonomy` commands for taxonomies declared as `taxonomies/<slug>.json` files: `push`, `pull`, `list` (every taxonomy on the site, its source and the post types it attaches to), `rm`, `diff` and `rollback`. Terms are never synced. `lps push`, `lps pull` and `lps validate` include them. Needs the matching plugin release.
